# FAQ

**Is RackPatch free?**  
Community Edition is Apache 2.0. Self-host without paying. Optional assisted pilots / hosted SaaS may charge a service fee.

**Do I need HOSTED_SAAS?**  
No. Leave it unset for Community Edition.

**Which LLDP formats work today?**  
Cisco NX-OS `show lldp neighbors detail` text. Other vendors are planned (Phase 9).

**Does missing LLDP mean the cable is down?**  
No. It means awaiting verification.

**How do I deploy on Railway?**  
See RAILWAY_DEPLOYMENT.md. Marketplace publish requires maintainer approval.
