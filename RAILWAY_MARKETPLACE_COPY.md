**Name:** RackPatch  
**Title:** RackPatch — Open Source Data Center Cabling Management  
**Tagline:** Plan, install, verify, and hand over data center cabling.  
**License:** Apache 2.0  

**Description:**  
RackPatch helps network and cabling teams import cable plans, track installation, compare Cisco NX-OS LLDP evidence to the design, require independent physical verification, and produce handover reports. Deploy into your own Railway account with PostgreSQL. Community Edition is free; optional assisted setup is a separate service.

**Do not claim:** NetBox, AI, fiber twin, or published template ID until real.

**Customer cost:** Railway usage-based billing on the customer’s project. Publisher does not host customer data.
